# NARIS Unity Design System

## Mode
Operate — gameplay HUD, pause flow, save/load, combat feedback.

## Tokens
- Ember Orange: #FF5A18
- Aether Violet: #7B61FF
- Mist Cyan: #55D9E8
- Ancient Gold: #C9A35A
- Ash Surface: #23272B
- Deep Void: #0B0D10

## Principles
- HUD must remain legible at 720p through 4K.
- Critical health/stamina/objective information stays in the top-left safe area.
- Pause/save/load controls are reachable by keyboard and gamepad.
- Mobile profile uses larger hit targets and touch-safe margins.
- Motion is restrained during combat; reduced-motion mode will suppress camera-heavy feedback.
