# NARIS Product Design Session — 2026-08-28

## Direction

The NARIS Toolkit interface is being revised toward a professional production workbench for artists, technical artists, designers, developers, and pipeline TDs.

## Visual exploration

- Ashen Industrial: dark, professional, focused, production-first.
- Aether Tech: technical, data-rich, developer-friendly, pipeline-oriented.
- Ember Forge: warm, immersive, artist-centric, especially useful for LookDev and creative modules.

## Current strategy

Use Ashen Industrial and Aether Tech as the production foundation. Apply Ember Forge selectively to creative areas such as LookDev, Materials, Lighting, and Render Preview.

The central viewport remains important, while the surrounding interface prioritizes asset metadata, validation, pipeline state, performance, logs, and actionable tools.

## Source artifacts

Production Bible, subtitles, dashboard HTML, viewer/game HTML files, and generated visual design concepts were used or produced during the session.

## Next step

Continue with a professional NARIS Workbench prototype after selecting the visual direction. The target must scale across creative and technical workflows and must not behave like a cinematic-only dashboard.
