# Higgsfield — Ashen Vessel Generation Contract V001

Use this document as the canonical generation brief for the Ashen Vessel character.

## Prompt

CALL OF NARIS — definitive heroic protagonist, Ashen Vessel. Battle-Hardened Noble / Arcane Warrior. Clearly good, noble, courageous, intelligent and protective; never evil, demonic, sinister, corrupted or villainous. Youthful mature male, strong square jaw, defined cheekbones, straight nose, pale healthy skin, clean-shaven, calm focused intelligent expression. Long thick silver-white naturally wavy hair reaching between shoulders and shoulder blades, swept back from the face, individual strands visible with layered silver highlights and darker silver shadows. Long deep rust-red/crimson/coral-red slit tunic with premium damask/jacquard botanical weave: repeating interlocking leaves and small floral motifs in a slightly darker red. Wide dark rich-brown leather waist belt with subtle weathering, fine edge stitching and simple polished bronze buckle. Narrow dark-brown leather baldric from right shoulder to left waist. Matching leather vambraces carrying restrained crimson textile motifs and small bronze buckles. Fitted dark charcoal/brown trousers. Tall rugged dark-brown riding boots to knee or slightly above.

Primary weapon: Flame Relic Sword, a clearly LONG heroic legendary sword, approximately 150 cm total length and 115 cm blade. Full-length blade visible from pommel to tip. Straight polished steel, subtle forged Damascus texture, precise central fuller, refined slightly curved engraved crossguard, tightly wrapped grip, prominent round decorated pommel. The weapon must never read as a short sword, dagger, cursed weapon or evil artifact. Restrained warm ember energy only.

Create a professional AAA game-development character turnaround: front full body, 3/4 hero view, side profile, rear view, face/hair close-up, complete sword profile, textile/leather/metal material close-ups and neutral turntable reference. Consistent exact character identity, anatomy, costume construction and weapon proportions in every view. Physically based materials, realistic textile weave, leather grain, metal micro-scratches, cinematic realism, premium dark-fantasy action RPG quality. Neutral studio presentation with controlled lighting.

## Negative direction

evil protagonist, villain, demon, horns, skull armor, excessive spikes, corrupted black armor, sinister glowing eyes, cursed weapon, short sword, dagger, oversized slab sword, generic medieval knight, cartoon proportions, anime proportions.

## Output acceptance criteria

- Heroic first-read.
- Silver-white hair clearly visible.
- Crimson damask fabric clearly visible.
- Primary sword visibly long and proportionally dominant.
- No evil/sinister facial read.
- Consistent character across all views.
- Suitable as source reference for Blender/Unreal production.
