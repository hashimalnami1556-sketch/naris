# Audio Production Checklist

## Source
- [ ] Provenance/license recorded for every production asset
- [ ] No placeholder labeled final
- [ ] Source master archived losslessly

## Music
- [ ] Loop boundaries click-free
- [ ] State transitions tested on beat/bar
- [ ] Boss phase transitions tested at boundary HP values
- [ ] Victory stinger cannot overlap active boss loop

## SFX
- [ ] 3–6 variations for high-frequency events
- [ ] Parry/dodge/telegraph remain audible under combat mix
- [ ] Spatial attenuation tested at gameplay distances
- [ ] Wolf identity remains spectral rather than ordinary animal Foley

## Ambience
- [ ] Regional snapshots transition without hard cuts
- [ ] Bone Beast arena removes inappropriate wildlife
- [ ] Ash Gate hum does not mask dialogue or interaction feedback

## Mix
- [ ] Master peak safety checked
- [ ] Dialogue ducking checked
- [ ] Boss ambience ducking checked
- [ ] UI remains legible without dominating cinematic mix

## QA
- [ ] Missing-event fallback is silent-safe and logged
- [ ] No broken references
- [ ] No accidental autoplay on menus
- [ ] Save/load restores appropriate music state
