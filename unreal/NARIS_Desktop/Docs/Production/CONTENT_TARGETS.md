# Content Expansion Targets
Production targets, not claims of finished art payloads:
- 20 hero/enemy/NPC character archetypes; 10 friendly / 10 hostile core roster.
- 99 biome variants assembled from reusable biome families.
- 6 campaign chapters with reusable encounter grammar.
- 30+ weapon/equipment archetypes, 120+ item definitions, 50+ crafting recipes.
- 40+ world events, 25+ codex/lore chains, 12+ boss/elite encounter templates.
- UI art, skeletal meshes, final animations, Niagara systems, audio masters and cinematics remain binary production assets and must be authored/imported into Unreal.
