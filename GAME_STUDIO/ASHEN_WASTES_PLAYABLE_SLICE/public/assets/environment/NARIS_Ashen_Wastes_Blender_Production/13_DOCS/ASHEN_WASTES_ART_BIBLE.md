# ASHEN WASTES — PRODUCTION ART BIBLE

Visual language: volcanic dark fantasy with basalt, obsidian, ash,
ancient stone/copper, ember-orange lava, violet aether, cyan mist
and ancient-gold accents.

Modular families:
Floors, walls, corners, columns, arches, stairs, ramps, bridges,
cliffs, rocks, lava rivers, caves, props, decals and VFX.

Material strategy:
Shared master materials + material instances. Variants include
worn, wet, soot-covered, cracked and molten.

Scale:
1 BU = 1 meter. Primary architecture grid = 2m.
Player reference = 1.83m.
Architectural pivots should normally sit at the bottom for predictable snapping.

Performance:
Use Nanite-ready geometry where appropriate. Keep collision simple.
Use LOD strategy for non-Nanite and distant background assets.
